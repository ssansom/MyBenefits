    Pen settings

    Defaults assume a 4-dose KwikPen where one full labeled dose is 60 clicks (so a 15 mg pen = 0.25 mg per click, 240 clicks total). If you prime before each injection, enter those clicks so they're subtracted too. "Extra clicks" lets you count any overfill you've confirmed for your pen; leave it at 0 to be conservative. Strength changes apply to the current pen; click settings apply to all calculations.

    Data is saved only in this browser on this device.

    Click counting to take partial doses is not part of the manufacturer's instructions, and clicks per dose can vary by pen. Use this only as a record-keeping aid for a plan you've agreed with your prescriber or pharmacist.
